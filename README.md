# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard
An interactive-style dashboard design for analyzing e-commerce sales performance, customer metrics, revenue trends, payment methods, and regional sales.

##  Project Overview
This project presents an e-commerce dashboard designed to bring important business performance indicators together in a single view.
The dashboard helps users explore sales performance, customer activity, revenue patterns, and product category performance.
 
##  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- **Total Orders:** 1,826
- **Total Sales:** 6K
- **Total Customers:** 825
- **Average Rating:** 3
- **Average Order Value:** 1.23K
- **Total Revenue:** 2243K

##  Dashboard Features
### 1. Sales by Product Category
  Compares sales across Electronics, Clothing, Home, and Beauty.
### 2. Daily Revenue Analysis
   Visualizes revenue fluctuations over the selected period.
### 3. Payment Method Analysis
   Compares payment methods such as cards, cash on delivery, and digital wallets.
### 4. Monthly Sales Trends
  Shows how sales change over time and helps identify potential trends.
### 5. Sales by Region
  Compares sales performance across West, North, East, and South.

### 6. Category Sales Map
  Provides a visual comparison of category-related values across the displayed periods.  
### 7. Interactive Filters
  The dashboard design includes filters for order date, product category, and payment method.

## Business Applications
This dashboard design can help users:
- Monitor key e-commerce performance indicators.
- Compare sales across product categories.
- Analyze revenue and monthly sales trends.
- Understand regional sales performance.
- Compare payment method usage.
- Explore business metrics using filters.
